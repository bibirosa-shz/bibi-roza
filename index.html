About

A practice built from a real life.

My path to teaching wasn't a straight line. It moved through community work in Canada, seven years and forty-plus countries of study, and time spent living in monasteries in India and Nepal — learning from lamas and, later, from western teachers Jack Kornfield and Tara Brach through the Greater Good Science Center at UC Berkeley.

Today I bring that mix of lived experience and grounded training to everyone I teach — whether that's one student or a room of a thousand.

  • Certified Mindfulness Meditation Teacher, GGSC UC Berkeley
  • Yoga Alliance 500-hr Yin Yoga Teacher
  • Co-founder, YogiBash & Bumi Festival
